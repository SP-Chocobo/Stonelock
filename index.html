How Stonelock Is Played

You are seizing assets. At the showdown you field any three cards from your final table layout. Matching cards earn flat bonuses; Interaction Stones warp the board. This table plays the commoner's valuation — Bread, Coin, Road high (3); Sword, Ferry mid (2); Quill, Crest, Chain low (1). Other regions value cards differently, and will arrive with their rule variants.

The Hand

  1. The Deal — five cards each from a fresh-shuffled pool.
  2. The Foundation — each player telegraphs a stone, then both commit 2 cards face-up.
  3. The Veil — second stone telegraphed (reverse order); both commit 1 card face-down.
  4. The Final Commitment — third stone telegraphed; 1 final face-down card. The leftover hand card is discarded dead.
  5. The Thinning — each player abandons one telegraphed stone, keeping two active.
  6. Stone Resolutions — in deal order, each player places their first stone, then their second.
  7. The Showdown — all cards flip; best 3 of your 4 are scored.

Scoring

Add the regional values of your three fielded cards. Pair (two identical): +2. Triad (three identical): +6. A phantom duplicate from a Red Stone counts as one of your three. Ties break by structure — Triad beats Pair beats Singles; identical structure is a Push and the marker holds.

The Interaction Stones

  • Red — Duplication. A phantom duplicate rides one of your cards, building toward a Pair or Triad with one physical card.
  • White — Lock. The card cannot be altered, stolen, or neutralized for the rest of the hand.
  • Blue — Exchange. Forcibly swap one of your cards for an unprotected opponent card. Face-up stays face-up; face-down stays face-down, but you may secretly inspect a hidden card that enters your layout.
  • Black — Disruption. Played on your turn like any other stone: it undoes the last stone effect upon a card — including unwinding a trade, with every riding stone traveling home. Cannot itself be undone, and cannot touch a locked card.

The Sovereign Honor Ledger

No betting at this table. After each showdown the Pivot Marker shifts by the net difference between the two scores. Push it the full distance onto your opponent's side to take the match. The Dealer Token rotates every hand.
